## Records

### The third-largest known twin primes

<p class="big-number">152748736824915 · 2<sup>480480</sup> ± 1</p>

A pair of primes two apart, each 144,654 digits long. They rank third on the PrimePages list of the largest known twin primes, found in January 2026 and proven prime. The PrimePages credit Vink and Brockwell, with TwinGen, NewPGen, the Twin Prime Search and PRST.

### World record: log(10) to 2.5 trillion digits

I computed the natural logarithm of 10 to 2,500,000,000,000 decimal digits with y-cruncher, announced in January 2026. It is the current world record in y-cruncher's records table. The computation took 166 hours and its verification another 147 hours, on a machine with two Intel Xeon 6230R processors and 192 GB of memory.

## What it is

I search for large primes of special forms and compute mathematical constants to record sizes. Both come down to very large multiplications, the right algorithms, and keeping long jobs running reliably on modest hardware.
