This site uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

It is hosted by Cloudflare, and Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits. On each page a small script sends Cloudflare the page's address, the site you came from, your browser type and how fast the page loaded. Cloudflare also sees your IP address, as any host does. I see only totals (visits, popular pages, countries), never who you are, and nothing is sold or shared.

To opt out, block scripts or use a content blocker: the site works the same without the script.
